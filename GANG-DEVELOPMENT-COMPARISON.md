# Gang equipment versus free development

Material equipment candidates costing more than 1% of spendable cash now pass a common whole-crew development comparison before purchase. Existing ascensions are applied and the crew re-read first. Both trajectories retain the same committed training, current equipment, prior purchases, native bonus time, warfare context and useful horizon. Temporary gear comparisons are clipped to the quoted useful lifetime.

The comparison evaluates continued development, short free training windows of 20/60/120/200/300 seconds, and, when available, eligible ascension-and-recovery windows of 60/120/200/300 seconds. Ascension alternatives come from the existing eligibility/availability checks and require an unchanged member signature less than five seconds old. They are hypothetical forecasts only. Applied ascensions, changed equipment/XP and protected work invalidate reuse. Manual assignments and no-training settings remain authoritative.

The equipment bill is charged once in the current objective's units. Equipment must produce more net development than the best completed free alternative, or provide a verified power advantage over it, while preserving milestone and warfare safeguards. Existing cash/augmentation readiness checks run first. Shared spending, batch/per-type budgets, reserves, native power checks and equipment lifetime guards still run before purchase.

Computation is bounded to three material candidate comparisons per planning pass, at most seventeen whole-crew trajectories. Normal operation allows three comparisons of up to 25 ms; repeated slow incomplete comparisons concentrate the allowance into one comparison of up to 50 ms within the equipment planner's existing time limit. Incomplete comparisons defer material purchases. Small-item fast paths retain their existing policies. Diagnostic records expose free-development kind, comparison count, cash penalty, projected output and rejection reason.

This is a bounded marginal comparison, not an exhaustive joint optimizer. Ascension choices already applied are represented in both trajectories. Material comparison slots rotate by stable member/item keys across passes, with a cursor restored only in the same reset. Budget-deferred candidates do not consume advancement, and duplicate power/production rankings share one slot. Additional 200/300-second windows are used only when no longer than half the available horizon. All previous freshness and completion requirements still apply.

## Adaptive effort and alternate tasks

Combat, hacking and charisma training can compete when their native task data is available. Besides the primary task windows, alternate tasks are evaluated at 60/120 seconds for ordinary training and 120 seconds for hypothetical ascension recovery. Manual work, no-training settings and committed recovery remain protected. Diagnostics identify the selected free-development task.

Timing history keeps at most eight valid observations from the last minute. Two slow incomplete searches among the last three observations switch to a single comparison with up to 50 ms. Successful or expired observations restore the normal three 25-ms slots. The actual time grant is clipped to the equipment planner's remaining 100-ms budget. No cached result becomes a purchase approval. Timing history is restored only within the same augmentation/BitNode reset.

Remaining opportunities include an early rejection once a completed free-development option already dominates the equipment purchase, and selecting alternate task windows from the next productive crime's stat deficits. Measured forecast-versus-output calibration is also still useful.
