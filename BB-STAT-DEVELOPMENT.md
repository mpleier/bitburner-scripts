# Bladeburner stat development

## Implemented

1. Installed Simulacrum allows a bounded gym/crime session alongside the selected ordinary BB mission when stats block progress or verified early preparation is active. The controller preserves the mission's tracked progress. The worker checks its activity lease, target/reset, installed augmentation, current mission identity/level/city, health, stamina, reserves and protected transitions. A mission completion ends conditioning early. Black Ops are never conditioning targets.
2. Early sleeve preparation compares observed success-chance readiness with measured rank ETA. It needs at least 30 seconds of narrow chance observations, the same target/reset/city, and rank ETA within 15 minutes. Unknown evidence retains normal assignments. Support and reputation workers remain protected; at least one eligible sleeve retains mission supply work.
3. Gym hash upgrades recognize adaptive gym assignments. Their benefit uses remaining, native-confirmed 30-second sleeve sessions plus a live player gym session. Legacy train-to targets use bounded native time-to-target estimates. Stale, reset, exited-owner and changed-task plans are excluded. Existing shared hash priorities and cash opportunity-cost gates remain.
4. Combat training scores include a discounted marginal action-speed benefit from dexterity/agility using the official continuous action-time factors. The term is disabled at the continuous one-second floor. The 25% discount is a planning heuristic, not a measured completion-time improvement; existing stamina-aware comparisons remain.
5. Player and healthy synchronized sleeves can compare gyms in Sector-12, Aevum and Volhaven. Relocation requires a material advantage over local alternatives after a one-second allowance, includes the $200,000 travel expense, and stays within 1% of unreserved cash. Native mutation checks preserve reserves and reject changed state. Local sleeve sessions retain ordinary shared spending limits.

Missing optional APIs/Formulas retain the existing safe work path. Only installed Simulacrum permits mission-parallel conditioning. Training quotas, rolling budgets, achievement restrictions, casino/reset/graft protection, manual work ownership, and home RAM headroom remain authoritative.

## Further improvements found

- Replace the discounted speed term with a measured end-to-end readiness objective, including stamina recovery and subsequent Black Ops.
- Rank gym hash upgrades against the specific skill-point upgrade or rank milestone they would displace, using observed XP improvements rather than only saved trainee time.
- Recover attribution of player gym/crime XP during parallel BB missions. These receipts deliberately do not calibrate external XP, since ordinary BB mission XP has not yet been independently subtracted.
- Use a measured travel/startup allowance and available sleeve stored cycles when comparing short sessions; the present travel delay is a conservative fixed planning allowance, not a measurement.

These are follow-up opportunities; no causal speed-gain percentage has been established.
