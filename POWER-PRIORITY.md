# Gang power priority

`gangs.js` now defaults to `--power-focus true`. Power development leads training, ascension and equipment choices until territory is complete, including after the ordinary safe-clash threshold is reached. Fixed-territory terminal power gain ranks qualified development plans before money or reputation utility. Recruitment and augmentation milestone checks, completed power payback, cash reserves, shared budgets, manual task overrides and clash safeguards still apply.

The power-priority mode can trade up to 50% of modeled gang production over its planning horizon for a verified power improvement, while retaining productive members. It starts once fresh rival power and a known power-update phase are available. Missing rival growth history does not block direct power optimization; safe-clash time savings are not claimed without measured rival growth.

`--power-focus false` restores balanced planning (including the earlier limited power objective below the safe-clash threshold). `--disable-adaptive-growth` retains legacy behavior. A money-focused crime selection can coexist with power-prioritized development.
