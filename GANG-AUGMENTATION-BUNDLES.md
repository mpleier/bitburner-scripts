# Gang augmentation bundles

The faction manager publishes current gang offerings, native prices, prerequisites, multipliers, owned/queued augmentations, and the committed purchase basket in its validated reset snapshot.

Reset planning compares pairs and triples from six route-relevant gang targets selected round-robin for total prerequisite benefit, price and time to unlock (at most 35 combinations). Prerequisite closures share each augmentation once and include otherwise undesired prerequisites. All dependencies must be owned, already in the committed basket, or supplied by the gang. Quotes include the committed basket, its donations, and current purchase inflation. A valid expensive-first dependency order gives a conservative price; it does not change the real purchase order.

Bundle benefits multiply the extra augmentations only. Existing queued and committed benefits are counted once. Combat routes use combat stat/XP and Bladeburner multipliers; hacking routes use hacking multipliers. Actual purchase and installation continue through their existing guarded paths.

Only fresh snapshots with repeatedly observed passive gang reputation justify bundle waits. The estimate uses the slower of observed and current native reputation rates. Cash and gang reputation accumulate concurrently. Zero/unknown funding, malformed data, missing prerequisites, cycles, overflow and waits beyond fifteen minutes reject the candidate. Already-affordable bundles remain the ordinary purchase planner's responsibility.

Each candidate enters the existing whole-basket install comparison, including capability recovery, income recovery, infrastructure rebuild, route horizon and the nonrenewable shared wait deadline. A bundle quote alone cannot authorize an installation.

## Gang production and weaker members

A fresh selected bundle overrides the individual-unlock goal for gang production. The reader requires a living autopilot owner, the same reset, matching validated augmentation ownership, an unexpired wait deadline, and available gang prerequisites. Invalid or stale bundle goals fall back to the existing individual goal. The chosen goal feeds the existing combined cash/reputation objective and training/ascension milestone guards; explicit focus flags and recruitment priority remain authoritative.

Training search now gives up to three early comparisons to the eligible member with the lowest projected production when that member contributes less than one quarter of the crew leader. The remaining search still rotates within the same simulation/time budget. This is an evaluation priority, not unconditional training. Completed whole-crew forecasts must still repay lost output, protect milestones and warfare, and leave productive capacity. Manual work, no-training settings, mandatory recovery and active training deadlines remain protected.

Further opportunities: compare forecast reputation/cash arrival against actual outcomes; rotate the early comparisons among several low-contributing members; select training tasks from the specific stat bottlenecks of newly unlocked crimes.
