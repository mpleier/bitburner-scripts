# Gang power equipment spending

Power-priority equipment may cost up to 5% of current spendable player cash per item when the projected warfare-readiness time falls by at least 20 seconds and 5%, the improved readiness is reachable within the equipment's useful horizon, and clash risk is zero. Other power purchases retain the 1% limit. Training-dependent development quotes retain their existing 1% limit.

The native purchase guard rechecks reset scope, quote freshness, gang power/territory, rivals, cash reserves, ownership and actual equipment stat gains. Larger quotes must have explicit power priority and readiness evidence. Their claimed immediate power gain cannot exceed the native candidate gain by more than 2%. Current training-dependent and active/residual-clash purchases cannot use the larger allowance.

The existing shared allowance, progression batch budget (normally at most 5% of cash), per-type budget, augmentation readiness guard and transient equipment lifetime guard still apply. This change expands qualifying items; it does not increase every spending budget or guarantee a purchase.

The direct equipment ETA comparison also preserves Infinity when warfare readiness is unreachable, preventing the wall-time converter's fallback from presenting it as zero.

Further opportunities: explicitly value purchases that change readiness from unreachable to reachable; compare power equipment against the next training/ascension opportunity using one common budget; measure predicted versus actual warfare-readiness savings before relaxing the thresholds.
