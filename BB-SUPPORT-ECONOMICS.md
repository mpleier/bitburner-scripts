# Bladeburner support and parallel-training follow-up

## Human replacement of sleeve support

The controller now compares recruiting one human with retaining the currently required support sleeves. Optional replacement requires:

- A verified useful squad, fresh native sleeve XP evidence, and healthy synchronized supporting sleeves.
- A verified stat bottleneck for the following Black Op while the current one is rank-gated. Native lookahead temporarily probes the following assignment and attempts to restore it synchronously; failed restoration invalidates the quote.
- A bounded planning window, reduced by known node-route or selected augmentation-install deadlines.
- Repayment of foregone player mission XP and any rank delay. Only half the released sleeve's estimated benefit is credited.
- Enough time for the released sleeve's first crime payout. No future paid gym or augmentation purchase is assumed.

The quote values the sleeve that the existing support allocator would actually release. It recruits one human at a time, preserving the useful human squad cap, recruitment budget, and all protected-work checks. A native start guard rejects stale/reset quotes, changed team/support counts, and a Black Op that has become ready. Sleeve assignments change through their existing allocator after a human actually arrives.

Unknown Formulas, optional APIs, incoming XP evidence or rank opportunity cost retain existing recruitment behavior. An approved recruitment creates a bounded, owned training goal for the specific released sleeve. It prevents a new reputation assignment from consuming that sleeve, respects existing protected work, expires within 15 minutes, and blocks overlapping replacement investments. The goal ends when the following operation becomes safe or its owning route/controller changes. This is a modeled payback comparison, not a measured speed guarantee.

## Parallel gym/crime XP measurement

The worker takes synchronous player/sleeve/BB endpoints during ordinary mission-parallel conditioning. It admits only short intervals with unchanged scope and no possible native mission completion, including a conservative five-second queued-work margin. BB success counters, action progress, duration and bonus-time changes supply additional rejection checks. Intervals containing potential failures/completions are excluded rather than estimating their XP.

Certified intervals subtract independently observed sleeve XP transfers. At least 15 seconds of valid exposure in one session is required; unknown background, interrupted work, reset/profile changes, overlaps or insufficient observations cannot calibrate. Samples only reduce the native gym/crime XP model and are capped at its predicted gain. Existing sample-count and exposure requirements still apply before calibration changes a future quote.

Receipts expose included/excluded time, fragment count and corrected XP. Ordinary mission XP is not credited as external training. Short missions can legitimately produce no usable measurement. Installed Simulacrum, activity ownership, reserves, rolling training limits and transition protections remain required.
